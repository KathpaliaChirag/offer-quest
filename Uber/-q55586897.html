A group is using a progarm to determine possible diverse deputations of 3 members for an upcoming conference. There are 'm' men and 'w' women who are eligible. A deputation is diverse only if it contains at least one man and at least one woman. Two deputations are considered distinct if one has a member that the other doesn't. Given a number of men and women, determine the number of distince ways to select a diverse deputation of 3 people.

EXAMPLE:

m = 1

w= 3

There is m=1 man available and w=3 women. Label them m1, w1, w2, w3 - There are 3 possible ways to form a diverse deputation: (m1, w1, w2), (m1, w1, w3), and (m1, w2, w3). The only other possible permutation is (w1, w2, w3) which doesn't include a man, so it is invalid.

use the function:

diverseDeputation(m, w)

int m = # of men

int w = # of women

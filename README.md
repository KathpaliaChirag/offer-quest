# Offer Quest

A company-wise collection of previous-year placement and interview questions to support your preparation journey.

## What's inside

Questions are organised by company wherever possible, so you can focus your practice on the roles and organisations you are targeting.

## Disclaimer

The material in this repository was compiled from publicly shared sources, including Telegram communities, Drive links, and other resources already available online. It is collected here solely to make placement preparation easier for students.

All credit and rights remain with the original creators and owners. If you own any material here and would like it credited, corrected, or removed, please open an issue or contact the repository maintainer.

## Contributing

Contributions are welcome. Please add only material that is publicly available and suitable to share, organise it by company, and mention the original source whenever you can.

Good luck on your offer quest! 🚀

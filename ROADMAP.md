# Offer Quest roadmap 🧭

The goal is simple: make placement prep less chaotic and more honest.

## Now

- Keep company folders searchable and consistently named.
- Add public OA patterns, interview experiences, and placement notes with source links.
- Keep the [index](INDEX.md) and [additional resources list](<Additional Resources/README.md>) current.
- Move files with unsafe names or unclear sharing status into `problem files` with a recovery note.

## Next

- Add lightweight company summaries: common round pattern, topic mix, and useful practice links.
- Add a small set of verified placement checklists for aptitude, DSA, CS fundamentals, and interviews.
- Improve duplicate detection and source metadata for new contributions.

## Always

- Prefer permissioned, public, shareable material.
- Credit the person or repository that did the work.
- Never include leaked questions, private data, paid reposts, or cheating tools.

Ideas and corrections are welcome through an issue or pull request.
